Manual

Task-oriented help for working with charts, time, workspaces, and application settings.

The Manual describes Kefer Astrology from the user’s point of view. You do not need to know how the application is implemented.

Begin

  • Getting started — understand the workspace and the main navigation.
  • Charts and workspaces — create, open, edit, save, and export your work.

Examine a chart

  • Chart views — move between the horoscope, aspectarium, information, revolution, and synastry views.
  • Transits and time — inspect a moment, navigate through time, and work with transit views.

Personalize the application

  • Settings and appearance — choose language, visual themes, glyphs, objects, aspects, and calculation defaults.

If you prefer to learn by exploring the interface, choose one of the Guided Tours.
