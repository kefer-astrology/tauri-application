Transits and time

Inspect changing positions and move a chart through time.

Open Transits to compare a selected radix chart with positions for another moment. Use its secondary navigation to choose the available transit operation.

Dynamics uses the same time-oriented part of the application for broader movement and range workflows. The controls distinguish a single instant from a generated time series.

When time navigation is available, choose a unit and amount before stepping backward or forward. Calendar-aware units such as months and years are applied as calendar changes rather than fixed numbers of seconds.

The selected chart remains the reference point unless the interface explicitly shows a temporary preview or transit overlay.
