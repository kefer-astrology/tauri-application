## Horoscope

The horoscope dashboard is the main calculated-chart view. Select a saved
chart, then use its wheel, object details, and context controls to inspect the
computed positions, house cusps, axes, and aspects. Clicking an object or
aspect opens its available detail; it does not change the calculation.

The wheel can be shown with the Ascendant or 0° Aries at its left edge. This is
a display preference only: it does not change the house system or any computed
value. See [Settings and appearance](../settings-and-appearance/#radix-wheel-orientation).

## Aspectarium

The aspectarium compares selected observable objects in a matrix or list. Use
its object and aspect filters to narrow the calculated aspects reported for the
selected chart. Choose an available cell or row to inspect the exact angle,
orb, and applying/separating state.

Empty cells mean no aspect was computed under the selected chart settings and
orb rules; they are not missing decorative data. The aspectarium can also show
a temporary transit overlay when one is open, but it does not calculate
aspect geometry in the browser.

## Spectrum

Spectrum is an overview of the selected chart in cards: the balance of
elements, modalities, house groups, quadrants and hemispheres, extraversion and
introversion, the aspect balance, configurations, stelliums, singletons,
retrograde planets and focal planets (chart ruler, final dispositor, angular and
unaspected planets). The wheel card adds the lunar phase, the lights above or
below the horizon and the Mercury and Venus phases; the highlights card picks
the chart's most notable facts.

Hover an item, a row or a highlight to show its planets on the wheel (and the
chart half it belongs to); click it to keep them shown, and click again to
release. A card shows a short note instead of values when the chart has not
been computed yet. As in the other views, a card's `⋯` menu removes it and
**+ Add** brings cards back.

## Revolution and synastry

**Revolution** currently provides setup controls for solar, lunar, and relative
return ideas, but submitting the form does not yet calculate or save a return
chart.

**Synastry** lets you choose or enter two participants and save a named analysis
setup. It can also collect a progression date for supported setup types. A
saved setup is not yet a computed synastry report or a second source of aspect
geometry.

For the objects, aspects, shapes, and configurations that current calculations
can report, see [Objects, aspects, and chart patterns](../objects-aspects-and-patterns/).
